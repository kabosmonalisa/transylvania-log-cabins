The idea

Small and private.

A wedding, a birthday, a retreat, a company day, even a classic-car weekend — whatever the occasion, it happens here, on one hillside surrounded by forest. Rares and Gabie keep every event small: never more than 100 people at a time.

How it works

Two ways to do it.

Fully hosted

They do it all.

Gabie cooks, and the tables, flowers and lighting are set up for you. You just turn up on the day.

Venue only

You bring your own.

Rent the estate on its own and bring your caterer, your planner, your vision. The space is ready; the rest is up to you.
