The idea

Small, by design.

Rares and Gabie open the estate for private gatherings — a wedding, a retreat, a long birthday dinner, a quiet company offsite, or something with no name at all. Never more than 100 guests, on one hillside, with nothing around you but forest and mountain. It can be almost anything, as long as it’s yours alone.

How it works

Two ways to do it.

Fully hosted

Leave it to them.

Hand over the food and the feel of the day. Gabie cooks — generous, seasonal, quietly unforgettable — and the tables, the flowers and the lighting are taken care of. You arrive, and it’s ready.

Just the venue

Or make it yours.

Prefer your own team? Take the estate on its own and bring your caterer, your planner, your vision. The place is yours for the day; the rest is up to you.
