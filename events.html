The idea

Small and private.

A wedding, a birthday, a retreat, a company day, even a classic-car weekend — whatever the occasion, it happens here, on one hillside surrounded by forest. Rares and Gabie keep every event small, never more than 100 people at a time, so the land stays quiet.
