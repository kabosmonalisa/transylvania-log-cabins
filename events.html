The idea

Small and private.

A wedding, a birthday, a retreat, a company day, even a classic-car weekend — whatever the occasion, it happens here, on one hillside surrounded by forest. Rares and Gabie keep every event small, never more than 100 people at a time, so the land stays quiet and the whole place feels like yours for as long as you have it. However you want the day run, there are two ways to do it.

Fully hosted

They do it all.

Tell Rares and Gabie what you have in mind, and they arrange the lot — catering, tables, flowers, lighting. You just turn up.

Venue only

You bring your own.

Rent the estate on its own and bring your caterer, your planner, your vision. The space is ready; the rest is up to you.
