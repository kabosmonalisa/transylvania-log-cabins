The people

A place made by hosts who live here.

Transylvania Log Cabins is the work of two people, Rares & Gabie, on a private estate near Peșteana in Hunedoara. What began as a single cabin has grown, over eleven years, into a small family of stays — cabins, treehouses and a barn — each its own world, all on the same quiet hillside.

They host it themselves, and it shows. More than 1,600 guests have stayed and left their reviews; the estate holds a 4.93 rating and Superhost status year after year. But the number they care about most is the small one: how few people are here at any time. Fewer guests, on purpose — because privacy is the thing you can't fake.

That's why it's adults only, and why the cabins sit apart from one another. Come for the quiet and you'll be left to it. Want more — a fire lit, a long dinner, a recommendation for the mountains — and they're a message away.

The estate

Built around the landscape, not on top of it.

Old and new structures, set among the trees rather than cleared into them. A heated pool and jacuzzi at every stay through the warm season, and forest or mountain in every window — the wild kept close, the comfort kept quiet.
